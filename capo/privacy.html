Capo Privacy Policy

Effective September 21, 2026

This policy describes the personal Capo deployment operated by Jacob L. Steenwyk. People running their own copy of Capo are responsible for their own configuration and data practices.

Information accessed

Depending on the connections you authorize and features you use, Capo accesses Gmail message headers, labels, message text, threads, and drafts; calendar lists and event details; and messages, images, and files you provide through Slack. Calendar details can include titles, times, locations, descriptions, and participants. OAuth credentials allow Capo to use the permissions you grant without receiving your Google password.

How information is used

Capo uses this information to carry out your requests and configured automations: email summaries and drafts, calendar management, reminders, planning, document preparation, and personalized briefings. Saved preferences, writing guidance, and past work help it provide relevant follow-ups. This personalization uses stored information; Capo does not itself train a general-purpose AI model on your Google data.

Processing and sharing

Relevant request context and tool results may be sent to the configured AI services, including Anthropic, OpenAI, or xAI, to perform these features. Replies and requested documents may appear in the configured Slack conversation, where people with access to that conversation can see them. Google receives API requests needed to read or change your mail drafts and calendar events. The exact services used depend on the deployment’s configuration.

These services have their own retention and account settings. The operator must configure providers consistently with the permitted use of Google data. Capo does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. Google data must not be used to train generalized AI models. Human access to Google data is limited to your affirmative consent for specific data, necessary security investigations, or legal requirements.

Capo’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage and retention

OAuth tokens are stored in private configuration on the machine running Capo. Conversation history, task records, documents, preferences, and execution records are stored in private runtime storage, separate from the public source code. External API connections use HTTPS. Local file permissions restrict access; this policy does not promise that every stored file is encrypted.

There is no single automatic deletion period for all saved records. Records may remain until the operator deletes them. Removing a preference or disconnecting an account does not erase historical records, backups, Slack messages, or records retained by service providers.

Your choices and deletion

You can disable integrations or scheduled work and revoke Google access through your Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized access but does not delete previously saved information or undo calendar and draft changes.

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of stored data, contact Jacob L. Steenwyk. Local records and backups need to be handled separately from Slack and provider records, which are subject to those services’ controls.

These web pages

The Capo pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. They do not request Google account access or include analytics scripts. The hosting provider may process ordinary web request information to serve and secure the pages. Other pages linked from this site may have separate features.

Changes

This policy will be updated when data practices change. Material new uses of Google data require updated disclosure and consent before that new use begins.
