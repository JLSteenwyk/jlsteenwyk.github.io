Capo

A personal AI assistant that helps turn everyday requests into finished tasks.

What Capo does

Talk to Capo in Slack to organize your calendar, research questions, prepare documents and email drafts, and manage coding work. Optional scheduled briefings bring together your day’s plans, follow-ups, and relevant news.

Connecting Google

With your permission, Capo can search and read Gmail messages to help with summaries, follow-ups, and drafting. It can create, edit, and delete drafts. Its current Gmail tools do not send email, although Google bundles sending with the permission used for drafts.

Capo can also discover calendars, read events, and create, update, or delete events on calendars you own. Connections and scheduled tasks are configured by the operator.

Your data

Relevant information may be processed by the configured AI providers and included in Slack replies. Private saved history, documents, and preferences support follow-up tasks. Read the privacy policy before connecting an account.

Availability and contact

This deployment is a personal project operated by Jacob L. Steenwyk, not a public signup service. The Capo source code is public; connected account data and credentials do not belong in that repository.

For access, support, or privacy questions, contact Jacob.
